Adds how often each train reaches your stop under 5 min late.

Based on

The same weekday is closer to your trip but has fewer runs. If it has too few late runs, the typical delay counts all days.

Loads train files from the Kurt site (or your own server), which sees your IP address and which trains you look at. Transitous gets start, destination, date, time, your IP address and an extension identifier that is the same for everyone. Chrome stores settings; Google syncs them if sync is on.
