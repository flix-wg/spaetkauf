Spätkauf Safari extension · iPhone · Deutsche Bahn

Buy the cheap ticket.
Ride the flex one.

Spätkauf picks the trains on your routes most likely to arrive 20+ minutes late and books the Sparpreis on them early, before any delay is posted. When the delay comes, the train binding is lifted: take a faster ICE, another route, or a later day.

Junction 2026 · Exa · Context.dev
01 · PREDICT Score every train you'd take Base rates from historic arrivals, plus fresh signals: construction notices, strike calls, storm warnings. Output: the chance of 20+, 60+ and 120+ minutes late at your destination.
02 · BOOK EARLY Buy while it still looks on time Only trips you'd make anyway, inside your budget, in your own bahn.de tab. Trains that already show a delay are skipped: a delay known at purchase doesn't count.
03 · UNLOCK Late? Pick a better train At 20+ minutes forecast you get a ping with faster or later options. At 60+ you choose: skip the trip for a full refund, or ride and get 25 % back.
THE RULES IT PLAYS BY · DB CONDITIONS OF CARRIAGE, 11 NOV 2026
≥ 20 min expected late at your destination, from a delay, a missed connection or a cancellation, even on a Sparpreis: ride the next or a later train, any route, a higher product class too (ICE), but no reservation-only trains. § 9.1.1 · bahn.de FAQ adds: earlier train the same day, later up to one year
≥ 60 min expected: don't start or abandon the trip and get the full fare back, Sparpreis included, by bank transfer. Or ride: 25 % back at 60 min, 50 % at 120, paid from €4. § 9.1.3 · § 9.2.1 · EU 2021/782 Art. 19
0–5 h scheduled arrival after midnight and 60+ late, or the day's last train cancelled: DB must offer onward transport. If it doesn't and you can't reach DB, a taxi up to €120 is refunded. § 9.1.5
known delays don't pay: no compensation if you were told before buying, and DB's FAQ counts rights from the delay known at booking. That's why Spätkauf predicts instead of reacting. Art. 19(9) · bahn.de FAQ
THE BET, IN ONE LINE upside = P(≥20) × (Flex − Spar)
       + P(≥60) × 25 % × Spar
       + P(≥120) × 25 % × Spar Example: 64 % · 22 % · 6 % on a €39.99 Sparpreis where Flexpreis is €149.90 → about €73 of upside, counting flex only if you use it. If it runs on time you lose nothing: you hold the cheap ticket you wanted anyway. If 60+ is forecast before you leave, you can walk away with a full refund.
